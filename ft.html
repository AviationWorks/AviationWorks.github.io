Fatigue

The FT code is a FOS add code, it is applied following a post-fatigue rest period. This page only captures those additions. Many fatigued trips will not appear here if they were handled solely through a fatigue removal code, which has its own separate code and is not captured by FT. Counts here should be read as a lower-bound indicator of fatigue activity, not a complete picture. We believe it is important to provide a comprehensive fatigue picture and will continue to work toward that goal.
