About This Site

This site displays statistics derived from APA MyOT Open Time data scraped daily. All data is aggregated — no individual pilot information is shown. Charts update automatically each day after the nightly scrape runs. Navigate using the menu above to explore Daily OT counts, PM (Premium Pay) sequences, and RF (Reserve) sequences.
